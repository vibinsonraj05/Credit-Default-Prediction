# 📂 Data Directory

This directory houses the dataset assets for the Credit Risk Intelligence Platform:

### Contents:
- **`raw/Credentials.csv`**: Synthetic authentication credentials for credit card customers (Email, Password, Name, Card Number, Account Number, Customer ID).
- **`raw/default of credit card clients.csv`**: Historical financial statements, repayment status (April - September), credit limits, and demographic attributes for 30,000 customers.

> **Note**: All records in these datasets are synthetic/anonymized for research and educational purposes.
